Today is August 19, 2023. I’ve been 16 for two days now, and I feel so much older than when I was 15. Anyway, this is my first post ever, so please read until the end.

Let’s begin today with a true story; a hook for you, the reader. This actually just happened.

I had to say bye to my friend Sean today. He’s simply a great person with a great head. When I went to say goodbye to him, he gave me some books. Some of these books were in Chinese, but I don’t think that he knows that I’m not adept at reading the language, much less, learning abstract ideas in Chinese.

![Books from a friend, including Show Your Work.](/images/legacy/read-first-initial-intentions/img_0100.jpeg)

Anyway, in this book titled _Show Your Work!_ by Austin Kleon, Sean left two notes. The first note read something about how this book doesn’t really mean much about anything, and the first line of the second note says: “OK, I take back what I have said.”

Right now, less than twenty pages into the book (with some pages just being pictures), I think the book can be life-changing for me. In fact, it is half of the co-inspiration that made me create this platform and start writing.

The other half of the co-inspiration is a part of Sean’s note:

![A handwritten note about sharing ideas so others can know you better.](/images/legacy/read-first-initial-intentions/img_1708.jpeg)

#### See Austin Kleon’s “Blackout Poetry” that I stole from him, which he stole from the 18th century.

I’m not sure how well this photo will turn out, but the part that I wanted to highlight says, “I wanted to know you better, in this way it can happen and the world can know you better.” Damn, Sean, this was a shot in the head.

I have always knew that I kept most of my ideas to myself, but I had never considered that there would be any big downsides that come because of it. But I now regret and acknowledge that it may have hindered every single one of my relationships. I mean, if everyone I knew knew a lot more about me, I would have so many open and honest bonds, ones that may have otherwise taken years to cultivate.

So I wanted to create this platform to allow you to better understand me. With more exposure to my ideas, my views, and my preferences, I hope that you will be able to strengthen your opinion of me, in a positive or negative direction. Either way, it will allow both of us to enjoy more time with the people that we like.

I hope to write honestly, freely, and often. I want everything to be thought out, and directed to you in a way that assumes that we’ve been long-term friends. My intention is to keep on posting as I go throughout life to see what I prioritized and thought about when I was younger. Today will be the start of this mess.

This will help improve my writing and typing skills, and it will serve as a place to get my ideas recorded. I also really hope that writing will help me sleep better, as I’ve been having trouble sleeping recently. Perhaps this will be where I’ll be when I can’t sleep?

I have this idea to add a time-relevant song to listen to while reading each post to share my music taste and potentially spread some nostalgia for those in the future. I’ll add all these song to some playlist on Spotify.

I plan to finish reading this book tonight and write tomorrow about how I plan to use it to impact my life. If everything goes smoothly, I urge you to read that post next, as I may use it to combat one of the biggest problems of my life: not being heard by others.

Finally, thank you for reading this. If this is your first time to try to get to know me, please know that I want to become lifelong friends with you.

Peace out,

Ben Suo
